title: Brown Butter Pumpkin Spice Cinnamon Rolls
categories:
  - Breakfasts
featured_image: 
recipe:
  servings: Makes ~8 rolls
  prep: About 2 hours, mostly rising
  cook: 350°F for 22-28 minutes
  ingredients_markdown: |-
    **Dough**

    * ¾ cup (180g) warm whole milk
    * 1 packet (2¼ tsp/7g) instant yeast
    * ¼ cup (50g) granulated sugar
    * ½ cup (120g) canned pumpkin purée
    * 1 egg, room temp
    * ¼ cup (57g) unsalted butter, melted
    * 2 tsp pumpkin spice
    * 1 tsp salt
    * 3⅓-3¾ cups (400-450g) all-purpose flour (start low and only add more, a little at a time, if it's too sticky)

    * ⅓ cup warm heavy cream, to pour over the rolls right before baking (the source used Good & Gather brown butter pumpkin heavy cream)

    **Filling**

    * ½ cup (113g) brown butter, cooled until soft/opaque (you can place it in the fridge to speed this up)
    * ⅔ cup (135g) packed brown sugar
    * 2 tsp cinnamon
    * 1 tsp pumpkin spice
    * Optional: 1-2 tbsp pumpkin purée for slightly more flavor

    **Icing**

    * 6 oz (170g) cream cheese, room temp
    * ¼ cup (57g) brown butter, cooled until soft/opaque
    * 1½ cups (180g) powdered sugar
    * ¾ tsp vanilla extract
    * ½ tsp pumpkin spice
    * Pinch of salt

  directions_markdown: |-
    **Dough**

    1. Combine the warm milk, sugar, and instant yeast. (If using active dry yeast, gently stir it and let it sit 5-10 min until foamy before proceeding.)
    2. Add pumpkin, egg, and melted butter, mix.
    3. Stir in salt, pumpkin spice, and flour.
    4. Mix until a dough forms.
    5. Knead the dough for 8-10 min.
    6. Place in a lightly greased bowl, cover, and let rise for 1-1½ hrs/until doubled.

    **Filling and shaping**

    1. For the filling, mix the soft brown butter, brown sugar, pumpkin spice, and cinnamon until combined/spreadable.
    2. Roll dough into a large rectangle on a lightly floured surface. Spread the filling evenly over it.
    3. Use a pizza cutter to first slice 8 pieces then roll them each up tightly. (Or you can tightly roll the whole thing then slice.)
    4. Place in greased 9x13 pan.
    5. Cover & let rise ~35 more min.

    **Baking**

    1. Pour warm heavy cream over rolls right before baking.
    2. Bake at 350°F for 22-28 min until golden.

    **Icing**

    1. Beat cream cheese and brown butter together until smooth.
    2. Then, beat in powdered sugar, vanilla, pumpkin spice, and salt until fully combined.
    3. Spread over the warm rolls.
    4. Enjoy!

I really like making pastries, and this one has quickly become one of our favorites to make together.
