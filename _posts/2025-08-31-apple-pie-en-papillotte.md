date: 2025-08-31
title: Apple Pie en Papillotte (in a Bag)
categories:
  - Pies
featured_image: /images/apple-pie-en-papillotte/apple_pie.jpg
recipe:
  servings: 6-8 Servings
  prep: 30 minutes
  cook: 90 minutes
  ingredients_markdown: |-
    **Crust**

    * 1 1/2 cups flour
    * 1 1/2 tsp. sugar
    * 1 tsp. salt
    * 1/2 cup vegetable oil
    * 2 Tbsp cold milk

    **Filling**

    * 4 cups peeled, thinly sliced apples
    * 1/2 cup sugar
    * 2 Tbsp flour
    * 1 tsp. cinnamon
    * 1 1/2 tsp. nutmeg

    **Topping**

    * 1/2 cup flour
    * 1/2 cup sugar
    * 1/2 cup cold butter

    **Vanilla Ice Cream**
  directions_markdown: |-
    **Crust**

    1. Preheat Oven 350 degrees
    2. Mix flour, sugar, salt, oil and cold milk together with a pastry blender, or food processor, until crumbly
    3. Press into a 9-inch pie plate; set aside

    **Filling**
    1. Combine apples, sugar, flour, cinnamon, and nutmeg together and pour into the pie shell

    **Topping**
    1. Mix flour, sugar, and cold butter together with a pastry blender, or in food processor, and sprinkle mixture on top of pie.

    **Bringing it Together**
    1. Place in a large brown paper bag (you can just ask for these instead of plastic when checking out at a groceyr store), seal with staples or pins and bake 90 minutes at 350 degrees
    2. Serve warm with vanilla ice cream :)

---
This yummy pie recipe comes from page 224 of one of Tracy's favorite recipe books, *A Pinch of Salt Lake*, which Matt and Tracy were given for their wedding. Grandpa Max Pyne loved this apple pie so much that one of the gifts he gave his married grandchildren was this apple peeler/corer/slicer. "So you can make me more apple pies" he told them with a twinkle in his eye. Delicious with vanilla ice cream, or as Grandpa Pyne used to like this pie, with canned (evaporated) milk!
