title: Pumpkin Cookies

  - Cookies
featured_image: /images/pumpkin-cookies/pumpkin-cookies.jpg
recipe:
  servings: Makes about 3 dozen cookies
  prep: Mixing and scooping
  cook: 350°F for 12 to 15 minutes
  ingredients_markdown: |-
    * 2 cups brown sugar
    * 1 cup oil
    * 2 cups canned pumpkin
    * 3 to 4 eggs
    * 2 tsp vanilla
    * 4 cups flour
    * 2 tsp baking powder
    * 2 tsp baking soda
    * 1 tsp salt
    * 1 tsp cinnamon
    * ½ tsp ground ginger
    * 1 tsp nutmeg
    * 2 cups chocolate chips

    A note on the eggs: the printed card doesn't list any, but there's a handwritten note on our copy that says "add 3-4 eggs." Three is what's been working, and they come out great.
  directions_markdown: |-
    **Mixing**

    1. Beat the sugar, oil, pumpkin, eggs, and vanilla.
    2. Blend in the dry ingredients.
    3. Stir in the chocolate chips.

    **Baking**

    1. Drop by teaspoonfuls on an oiled baking sheet.
    2. Bake at 350°F for 12 to 15 minutes.
---
These pumpkin cookies came to us on a recipe card signed Angela Graham. The printed list doesn't call for any eggs, but there's a handwritten note in the margin that says "add 3-4 eggs" — three is the number that got used, and they came out great. The card makes about three dozen.
