  ingredients_markdown: |-
    * 1 c sugar
    * 1 c corn syrup
    * 1 c peanut butter
    * 6 c rice crispies
    * 1 c chocolate chips
    * 1 c butterscotch chips
  directions_markdown: |-
    1. Bring sugar + corn syrup to boil.
    2. Take off stove, add peanut butter.
    3. Pour over crispies, put in a 9x13 greased pan.
    4. Melt both chips together and spread over top.

This card is written out in my father-in-law Matt's handwriting, and these are a staple at Spackman family gatherings. Grandma Spackman always had several pans of them in her freezer, and her friends brought them over too, so there was never a shortage. You could have one any time you stopped by to visit Grandma and Grandpa. She loved chocolate, and our family has always loved chocolate and peanut butter together.
