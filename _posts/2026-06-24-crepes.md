This crepe recipe is enjoyed at every Spackman Family General Conference brunch twice a year, and sometimes even more frequent. You can put Nutella, fruit, peanut butter, or generally anything sweet inside of them. A family classic is canned cherry pie filling... though whether it should still be considered a reasonable filling it up for debate.
