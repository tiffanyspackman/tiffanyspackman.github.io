recipe:
  servings: Makes 36 rolls
  prep: 1 hour
  bake: 7-8 minutes
  ingredients_markdown: |-
    * ½ cup shortening 
    * ½ cup sugar
    * ½ tsp salt 
    * 1 cup hot water 
    * 2 eggs
    * 1 pkg (2 ¼ tsp) yeast 
    * 4 cups flour 
    * Melted butter

  directions_markdown: |-

  **Prep**
    1. Make the dough the night before. Mix together shortening, sugar, and salt. 2. Pour hot water over this and let sit until lukewarm. 
    3. Beat in 2 eggs. 
    4. In a small bowl, dissolve yeast in a couple tablespoons of water. Then, add yeast to the rest of the mixture. 
    5. Add flour, 1 cup at a time. Leave this in the bowl until it has doubled in size. 
    6. Take fist and poke down. 
    7. Cover bowl with saran wrap and put in the fridge overnight. 
    
    **Rolling out**
    1. 3 ½ hours before eating the rolls, take the dough out of the fridge and divide the dough into 3 sections. 
    2. Use flour to roll each section into a pie shape. 
    3. Cut into 12 wedges. 
    4. Roll into crescent shape, starting at the wide end first. 
    5. Place on greased or parchment-lined cookie sheets and let rise until doubled in size (3 ½ hours). 

    **Baking**
    1. Bake at 375° for 7-8 minutes in a hot oven. 
    2. Brush with melted butter.
  ---
These are Grandma Spackman's famous butterhorn rolls, which are enjoyed at almost every family dinner. They're especially good for big dinners such as Thanksgiving and Christmas. With every roll you eat you can feel a little bit of Grandma's love for you.
