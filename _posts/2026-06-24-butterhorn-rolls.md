title: Grandma Ann Spackman's Butterhorn Rolls

featured_image: /images/butterhorn-rolls/butterhorn-rolls.jpg
recipe:
  servings: Makes 36 rolls
  prep: 1 hour
  cook: 7-8 minutes
  ingredients_markdown: |-
    * ½ cup shortening
    * ½ cup sugar
    * ½ tsp salt
    * 1 cup hot water
    * 2 eggs
    * 1 pkg (2 ¼ tsp) yeast
    * 4 cups flour
    * Melted butter

  directions_markdown: |-
    **Prep**

    1. Make the dough the night before. Mix together shortening, sugar, and salt.
    2. Pour hot water over this and let sit until lukewarm.
    3. Beat in 2 eggs.
    4. In a small bowl, dissolve yeast in a couple tablespoons of water. Then, add yeast to the rest of the mixture.
    5. Add flour, 1 cup at a time. Leave this in the bowl until it has doubled in size.
    6. Take fist and poke down.
    7. Cover bowl with saran wrap and put in the fridge overnight.

    **Rolling out**

    1. 3 ½ hours before eating the rolls, take the dough out of the fridge and divide the dough into 3 sections.
    2. Use flour to roll each section into a pie shape.
    3. Cut into 12 wedges.
    4. Roll into crescent shape, starting at the wide end first.
    5. Place on greased or parchment-lined cookie sheets and let rise until doubled in size (3 ½ hours).

    **Baking**

    1. Bake at 375° for 7-8 minutes in a hot oven.
    2. Brush with melted butter.
---
These are Grandma Ann Spackman's butterhorn rolls, and they showed up at just about every family gathering we have — Thanksgiving, Christmas, General Conference, all of it. Grandma Ann was Sam's grandma, and since she passed away this summer these rolls have felt even more special to us. The whole family makes them now for our big dinners, and every batch brings back those gatherings and puts a smile on everyone's face.
