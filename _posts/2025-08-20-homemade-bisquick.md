title: Homemade Bisquick Mix
categories:
  - Bread
featured_image: 
recipe:
  servings: 
  prep: 15 minutes
  ingredients_markdown: |-
    * 8 cup flour
    * 1/4 cup + 2 tsp baking powder
    * 4 tsp salt
    * 1/4 cup sugar
  directions_markdown: |-
    1. Use a pastry blender to mix all the ingredients together. 
    2. Store in an airtight container in the fridge. 
    3. Use in place of store bought bisquick mix in biscuits, cornbread, etc.
